About the atlas & sources

Select a word to read its etymology or dictionary record in the sidebar. Tap +x to explore nearby languages. Separate ↗ links open the actual source. Colours group explicit etymological sources, not language families.

Research dictionaries add source-specific IDS and WOLD meanings. Forms, varieties, credits, and annotations are preserved; no automatic Wiktionary alignment or cognacy is claimed. Dictionary licenses & import notes ↗

WALS adds language profiles and typological context; it does not supply word etymologies or territory boundaries. Missing coordinates never remove a word from the results.
