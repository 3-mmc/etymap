About the atlas & sources

Tap a count to explore nearby languages. Word links open Wiktionary; the locate button finds a word on the map. Colours group explicit etymological sources, not language families.

WALS adds language profiles and typological context; it does not supply word etymologies or territory boundaries. Missing coordinates never remove a word from the results.
